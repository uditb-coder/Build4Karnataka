## SECTION 1B — 🔍 INITIAL SCREENING & SELECTION CRITERIA

To ensure high prototype quality on event day and maintain optimal floor logistics at JP Nagar Innovation Hub (~100–120 participants), all 50+ applicant teams undergo an online screening round evaluated by **Udit** and **Gururaj** on **August 22–23, 2026**.

### 1. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Audience:** Engineering students (1st–4th year BE/B.Tech), MCA students, and recent passouts (0–6 months) from VTU-affiliated and COMEDK member colleges across Bengaluru.
- **Team Size:** 3 to 4 members per team (inter-branch and inter-college teams permitted).
- **Registration Fee:** ₹399 per team (inclusive of 18% GST). Payment UTR verified upfront by Aditi before screening.
- **Prerequisite Skill Mix:** At least 1 member must have basic familiarity with prompt engineering, web/UI design, or no-code tools (Glide, Voiceflow, Bubble, Make, Airtable, OpenAI/Gemini APIs).

---

### 2. Online Screening Submission Fields (Collected at Registration)
1. **Selected Domain:** Dropdown choice of 1 out of 5 domains (Healthcare, EdTech, FinTech, Retail, Smart City).
2. **Problem Statement & Idea Pitch (100–150 words):** Summary of the specific pain point the team aims to solve.
3. **Proposed GenAI / No-Code Tech Stack:** Tools intended to be used (e.g., *Voiceflow + OpenAI GPT-4o API + Airtable*).
4. **Team Complementarity & Roles:** Brief breakdown of team member responsibilities (e.g., *Lead Builder, UI Specialist, Pitch Presenter*).
5. **Past Portfolio / GitHub Link (Optional):** Optional link to past hackathon builds or personal projects.

---

### 3. Screening Evaluation Rubric (100-Point Matrix)

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring Parameters | Evaluator |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Alignment** | **30 Points** | Is the problem real, specific, and clearly aligned with the chosen domain? Does it address a genuine user pain point in Karnataka/Bengaluru? | Gururaj |
| **GenAI / No-Code Feasibility** | **40 Points** | Is the proposed technical approach buildable in 6 hours using AI/no-code tools? Is the scope realistic for a 1-day buildathon? | Udit |
| **Team Skill Complementarity** | **30 Points** | Does the team have a balanced mix of technical building, design, and presentation capabilities? | Udit + Gururaj |

---

### 4. Selection Cut-Off, Shortlist Announcement & Waitlist SOP
- **Shortlist Cut-Off:** Top 25–30 teams scoring highest on the 100-point rubric are selected.
- **Official Announcement (Aug 24 at 12:00 PM):**
  - **Selected Teams:** Receive official confirmation email + WhatsApp invite to JP Nagar Hub with Team ID & Entry Pass.
  - **Waitlist Teams:** Next top 5 teams placed on standby. If any selected team fails to confirm attendance by Aug 25 (6:00 PM), waitlisted teams are auto-promoted in rank order.
